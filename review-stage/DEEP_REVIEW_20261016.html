Method Description

The direction measures ACTIVE leakage from a sequence of final outputs, not a single fixed release. A cooperating analysis partner holds an "own" genotype panel and varies its own input across re-fits of a joint PCA with the honest study. Each re-fit returns a truncated final output — the top-k joint eigenvectors V_t and eigenvalues Λ_t — which the partner (who also knows its own scatter K_t = Z_P(t)^T Z_P(t)) converts into Y_t = V_t Λ_t − K_t V_t. The central identity Y_t = C_H V_t is exact: each output is a linear measurement of the honest sample scatter C_H along the released directions. Stacking q outputs gives C_H Q = Y over Q = [V_1..V_q], so the range of Y recovers the span-restricted operator C_H P_Q and the projection norm onto that range is a study-inclusion score. Input variation is an AF-preserving block exchange between two of the partner's own rows, which leaves every per-SNP histogram, allele frequency, variance and HWE/missing count unchanged while moving individual structure. Three component studies make the mechanism verifiable, and the adversarial ablation in s65 (raw-eigenvector stacking reproduces nothing) plus the no-release control in s66 (known calibration-member genotypes at chance under three preprocessing choices) isolate the effect to the tomography identity and the release, not to the partner's own data. Data flow: real 1000G chr22/chr1 prefixes → disjoint 5-role split (honest/partner/non-member/reference/shadow) → joint-PCA re-fits → stacked recovered operator → held-out-half-oriented membership AUROC and TPR@5%FPR vs information-matched passive baselines (conditioned AF, conditioned single release) → per-split JSON → results tables.
