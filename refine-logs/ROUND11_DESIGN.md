# Round-11 (limitation-closure) — experiment designs

Four sub-agents designed these against the current repo (14-pp paper). Each
closes one honest limitation of `paper/main.tex`. Script numbering continues the
existing `sNN` scheme; earlier proposals collided on s37/s38, resolved here.

## Bybiobank-scale real-haplotype cohort — `code/semi_synthetic.py` + `code/s37_realsynth_scale.py`
**Limitation closed:** "the real-data arm remains capped at the 1000G sample
set" (Discussion) + the §scale boundary claim that PC-dominance is a small-(n×M)
phenomenon. We cannot get a real biobank panel, but we can build a large cohort
with REAL haplotype structure.
**Method:** Li–Stephens haplotype copying (HAPGEN2-style mosaic resampling)
from the real 1000G phased library (`real_phased.py`), a single vectorized
numpy routine. Per target super-population, copy with a per-marker switch
probability `1-exp(-rho·gap_bp)`; two copied haplotypes per individual. Chosen
over `simulate_population_host` (its parametric LD is what is in question) and
over msprime (a demographic model cannot preserve the observed 1000G
haplotype background).
**Labels without circularity:** all arms generated by the identical procedure;
membership assigned AFTER generation by the same 3-way permutation as every
other experiment.
**Positive controls (mandatory):** (A) LD decay r²(d) matches 1000G (computed
directly with a numpy bin estimator in `semi_synthetic.ld_decay`) — calibrates the
switch rate; (B) AF spectrum
matches donor library; (C) pairwise-LSR relatedness ≈ real (guard against
shared-donor IBD inflation); (D) top-10 eigenvalue fraction ≈ real.
**Measurement:** identical to `s27_gpu_scale.cell` (AF-only, train-half PC,
combined, `pc_refbasis`, `pc_eigonly`, in-sample gap, fst_like), on GPU
(`/opt/miniforge/bin/python3`).
**Grid (delivered):** nsp ∈ {150, 500, 2000, 8000, 10000, 12000} × M ∈ {8000,
10000, 6000}, one marker density per cell, 3 seeds. (An initial draft planned a
larger grid with nsp=33000 and a thin/dense split at τ=1500/300; those cells
exceeded the 24 GB GPU at the Gram-eigenvector step and were replaced by the
delivered six cells.)
**Claim tested:** at biobank n with real haplotype structure, does AF still
lead (retire "just too small") or does PC≥AF revive (revise the boundary)?
**Risk:** shared-donor IBD inflation — guarded by controls A/C and by reporting
the IBD/LD channels separately.

## 2. Per-individual privacy metric — `code/s38_privacy_metric.py`
**Limitation closed:** every claim is an AUROC (rank statistic); no absolute
per-individual privacy quantity. A privacy reviewer asks "what does 0.88 mean
for one person?"
**Metrics:** (i) per-individual membership advantage `A* = max_t(TPR−FPR)`
(= KS distance; ≠ 2·AUC−1 for skewed score distributions, and we report the
gap); per-individual posterior `ρ_i` from a score-density likelihood ratio
(Sankararaman/Homer framing); (ii) empirical f-DP `(ε,δ)` lower bound
`ε_lb(δ) = max_α log((TPR(α)−δ)/α)`, validated against the `g_{ε,δ}`
trade-off. Rejects k-anonymity (aggregate releases → not well-defined).
**Output:** re-express the frontier (`s1c`), PC channel (`s2b`/`s10`), boundary
(`s5`), governed contrast (`s23`) in `(AUC, TPR@1%, A*, ε_lb, median ρ, LR_max)`,
with the reference-fitted structure control asserted at `A*≈0, ε≈0`.
**Cost:** CPU, <10 min — re-runs the scores (per-individual vectors were never
stored) at the existing operating points.
**Claim added:** "releasing the study-fitted loadings gives every member a
per-individual advantage of A* and an empirical ε≥X; the governed release drops
both to the no-release floor."

## 3. Governance enforcement + bundle leak — `code/enforce.py` + `code/s39_enforcement.py` + `code/s40_bundle.py`
**(A) Sole-egress binding, built.** Current monitor is a plaintext library call
a study author can bypass. `s39` demonstrates the bypass (`open(...,'w').write(
loadings)` succeeds against the library prototype) then closes it with a
minimal process-isolation runtime: child study process with a PEP-578
`sys.addaudithook` denying write-opens/sockets/subprocess/ctypes, its ONLY
writable channel a pre-opened pipe fd wired to the parent's `EgressMonitor`
(second enforcement point). Host verified: `unshare`/`bwrap` unavailable, the
audit hook reliable. Threat model = A2 negligent author (not root, no native
code), matching the paper. Positive control (undeclared release blocked, file
never created); negative control (declared AF@1dp + eigenvalues flow, chain
verifies, s23 governed tier reproduced). CPU, <1 min.
**(B) Bundle leak.** `s40` measures the FULL aggregate of a realistic
multi-object bundle (pooled AF@2dp + per-site AF@2dp + top-10 eigenspectrum +
λ_GC + call-rate table (s20 M3) + top-50 p-values) with a joint attacker, vs
the strongest single object; leave-one-out attribution; structural-zero
falsification control (eig/λ_GC/pvals alone at chance); single-object governed
negative control. CPU, minutes.
**Claims:** the binding statement moves from argued to built; the per-channel
analysis under-estimates the bundle, and an anti-composition clause must cover
orthogonal channels, not only AF-vs-loadings.

## 4. Multi-chromosome phased IBD + realistic missingness — `code/genetic_map.py` + `code/s41_phased_ibd_genome.py` + `code/s42_missingness_realistic.py`
**(A)** Extend `s36` from chr22/marker-length/single-density to chr1+chr2+chr22
at full marker density, with the IBD statistic in **base-pairs / centimorgans**
(real genetic map, `numpy.loadtxt` of the 1000G IMPUTE2 map) and a length
threshold sweep {0.5,1,2,3,5 cM}. Planted-IBD positive control + self-exclusion
kept. Needs new full-marker phased caches for chr1/chr2 (`get_real_phased(c,
ld_window=1, max_snps=200000)`; one-time ~20 min parse). Risk flagged: LSR
saturates at larger released-set sizes — reported honestly, not tuned away.
**(B)** Replace `s20`'s hand-planted per-marker missing-rate gap with a
model-based realistic missingness arm: real 1000G dosage skeleton + real
population-level batch proxy (`1000g_panel.txt` `pop`) + depth/quality-driven
missingness, where the member-correlation is an explicit batch-composition
parameter `ρ ∈ {0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1}` (not an individual-level Δ). Sweeps
`ρ × base-missing × σ_batch × window`, with ρ=0 null, holdout self-reference
control, and `super_pop`-vs-`pop` ancestry-confound control.
**Claims:** the haplotype bound becomes multi-chromosome/multi-density/
physical-length; the missingness channel becomes a monotone curve in an
interpretable batch parameter instead of a "≤1.00" ceiling.
