CPU-only measurement study: membership-inference leakage of released statistics
in multi-site GWAS. Steps:
  S0 sanity: pooled lambda_GC inflated uncorrected, ~1 after PC correction; a
    planted signal detectable; AF-LRT attack beats chance; noise ~chance.
  S1c frontier: study members vs INDEPENDENT non-members (disjoint from study AND
    from the attacker's reference panel), ancestry-matched; tiers F2(top200)/
    F4(AF 1dp)/F3(AF 2dp)/F5(full)/F5-per-site; 5 seeds; bootstrap CI + ancestry
    control + per-ancestor AUROC.
  S2c mechanism: cross-validated logistic meta-attack over per-target features
    (base AF-LRT score + PC projection distance/alignment); controls T2 (loadings
    refit on attacker reference -> out-of-sample), T3 (study-mean centering),
    permutation; 5 seeds.
  S3b paradox: scripted analyst, stopping rule COMPUTED from revealed spectrum,
    quality MEASURED by re-running the association pipeline; 2 families x 3 seeds.
  S4 msprime cross-check; S5 site-size x precision boundary; S6 dimension sweep.
