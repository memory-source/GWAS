## What the results do not support
- No real low-structure large-n cell — whether AF beats PC at Fst≈0 given size is undetermined on real data; all large-n rungs have Fst≥0.075. Round 15 (s51) attributes this to the 1000G single-super-population pool cap: the pooled structure-zero arm still has PC>AF at n_study=167–220, while a fixed-superpopulation-proportion ladder reaches AF-first at ~constant Fst≈0.09 by ~n=400, so the real switch that can be exhibited is a joint-size crossing, not a zero-structure one. Round 16 (s56, fixed-reference design) grows the structure-zero study to n_study=350 with the reference held at n_ref=150 (AFR); the pooled PC>AF gap remains positive at every study size tested (AFR n350 chr22/1/2 +0.102/+0.140/+0.181, sd 0.018–0.037) with basis controls near chance (0.37–0.63), so the real structure-zero switch is above the 1000G single-population pool with a full-size reference; the 1000G-derived crossover is unobserved.
- The eps_lb quantity is NOT a statistically valid f-DP lower bound (no confidence treatment of selected ROC rates/thresholds) and the ε summary is not order-preserving with AUROC (AF full AUROC 0.845 > AF@2dp 0.840 but ε 4.120 < 4.190). Reported as an in-sample, resolution-limited descriptive summary only.
- The phased haplotype channel is not bounded in general; only the tested longest-shared-run / IBS estimators fail to separate unrelated 1000G members.
- The eigenvalue-only negative is a random-basis projection ablation, not a proof that no eigenvalue-dependent attack exists.
- S37 large cells use a subsampled PCA fit (≤20000 rows).
- The GPU-port harness `common_gpu.auroc_gpu` assigns arbitrary distinct ranks to exact ties (a constant score returns 0.0, not 0.5); the archived s27–s36 cells are dense (measured tie mass 0.0 at a representative cell, s59), so the checked precision is unaffected, but the kernel is not tie-correct.
